## Video direction

- **palette system** — from `frame.md`: ink-black stage (#000000 / #1A1918) with a vignette to pure black; fire-orange #CE5909 is the ONLY key light and accent (rim glow, ring, light sweeps, embers); cream #E7D8C3 for display type; brown #843906 only inside photos/logo. Glow is orange light on black, never a new hue.
- **type** — Cormorant Garamond 700 lowercase for every statement; Montserrat 800 for the timer digits only; Montserrat 500 wide-tracked caps for small labels ("introducing", flavor names, URL).
- **motion grammar** — cinematic and slow: long-tail eases (power3/expo out), slow camera moves (dolly, push-in, forward flight) running under every photo shot; type fades/blur-ins with a soft glow bloom rather than slides; impacts land as one hard cut or a short scale-settle on a bass hit. Reveals are cued to the music's hits (no narration): one element per hit, never the whole frame at t=0.
- **atmosphere** — sparse drifting embers and soft steam on photo frames; screen-blend light sweeps (max one per frame); film-grade vignette on every frame.
- **rhythm / held frames** — Frame 1 is a near-still breather (only an ember and fades); Frame 3 holds the logo still for its last second before the push; Frame 6 ends on a held end card with only a slow push-in. Frames 2, 4, 5 carry the energy.
- **negative list** — no flat poster color fields, no rounded cards, no bouncy/elastic eases, no stock photos, no redrawn logo; no slideshow (front-load then freeze) and no screensaver (many elements floating independently — embers are the only ambient drift).
