## Video direction

- **palette system** — from `frame.md`: ink-black #000000 and fire-orange #CE5909 as the two full-bleed grounds (alternate them shot to shot for punch); cream #E7D8C3 for type on black, ink-black for type on orange; brown #843906 only inside photos/logo. Glow #FF8A3D allowed for rings/lights only.
- **type** — headline slams in Montserrat 800 UPPERCASE (tight tracking) for punch words; Cormorant Garamond 700 lowercase for the brand-voice lines ("golden.", "crisp.", "find a store."); Montserrat 500 wide-tracked caps for small labels.
- **motion grammar** — FAST. Every shot enters on a hit: slams land with a short deterministic shake, words snap (no slow fades), photos punch in (scale-in 1.25→1.0 in ~0.3s) then keep a slight drift. Exits are the next cut. Eases: expo/power4 out for snaps, a little overshoot allowed on slams only. One thing changes on screen at least every 0.5–1s.
- **graphics layer** — animated tech grid (dynamic-grid) on black grounds, thin orange rings/brackets/hairlines that draw on, RGB-split on photo punches, brief white flashes and light leaks at section boundaries.
- **rhythm** — frames 1–6 are a rapid build (≤2.4s each); frame 7 is the one "hero" feature beat (3.5s); frames 9–11 are fast list/proof beats; frame 12 holds a little longer as the end card.
- **negative list** — no slow fades, no long holds over ~1.5s except the end card, no rounded-candy UI, no stock photos, no redrawn logo; no slideshow (front-load then freeze) and no screensaver drift.
